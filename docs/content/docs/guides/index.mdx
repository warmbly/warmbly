---
title: Getting started
description: Connect a mailbox, warm it up and send your first campaign.
---

Warmbly sends cold email from mailboxes you own. It warms them up, spreads your campaigns across them, and brings every reply into one inbox.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Connect your mailboxes

Open **Accounts** and choose **Add account**. Connect Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, or any host over SMTP and IMAP.

![Add account: Google, Microsoft, any SMTP and IMAP host, a file import, or an inbox vendor](/guides/add-account.webp)

[More about mailboxes](/guides/mailboxes/)

</Step>

<Step>

### Check your domains

**Sending domains** shows whether SPF, DKIM and DMARC are published for every domain you send from.

![Sending domains: each domain with its mailboxes, authentication and tracking host](/guides/sending-domains.webp)

[More about sending domains](/guides/sending-domains/)

</Step>

<Step>

### Turn on warmup

New mailboxes start with warmup off. Switch it on from the mailbox's **Warmup** tab. Warmup is part of every paid plan.

![A mailbox's Warmup tab: participation, today's sends against the ramp target, pool standing and the ramp settings](/guides/mailbox-warmup.webp)

[More about warmup](/guides/warmup/)

</Step>

<Step>

### Send a campaign

Open **Campaigns**, choose **New campaign**, write the emails, add contacts and pick the sending hours.

![The Steps canvas: the first email, then follow-ups that reply in the same thread, each with its wait on the line](/guides/campaign-steps.webp)

[More about campaigns](/guides/campaigns/)

</Step>

<Step>

### Answer the replies

Every reply, from every mailbox, lands in the **Inbox**. With [inbox tagging](/guides/inbox-tagging/) turned on, replies are also labelled by what the person wants.

![The unibox: folders, mailboxes and labels on the left, the conversation list, and the open thread](/guides/unibox.webp)

[More about the inbox](/guides/unibox/)

</Step>

</Steps>
